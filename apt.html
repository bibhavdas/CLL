  • ▶

  • ▶
    1901–1955 = 55 years: 55÷4=13 leap years (+1 day each). So 55+13=68 extra days. 68÷7=9 rem 5.
  • ▶
    Jan 1956 (leap year): Jan=31→3 odd days, Feb 1–18: 18 days → 4 odd days.
  • ▶
    Total odd days = 0+6+5+3+4 = 18 → 18÷7 = 2 rem 4.
  • ▶
    0=Sun,1=Mon,2=Tue,3=Wed,4=Thursday
